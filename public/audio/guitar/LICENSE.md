# Sample licence and attribution

The `.mp3` files in `acoustic/` and `electric/` are rendered from the
**FluidR3_GM** soundfont by **Frank Wen**, taken from
[gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts).

- `acoustic/` — FluidR3_GM program 25, *Acoustic Guitar (steel)*
- `electric/` — FluidR3_GM program 27, *Electric Guitar (clean)*
