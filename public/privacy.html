The microphone

The tuner and the “play this note” checks in lessons listen through your microphone so the app can tell which note your instrument is playing. The microphone only turns on after you tap Start tuner or Listen for me, and it turns off when you leave that screen.

The sound is analysed on your device, a few times a second, to estimate its pitch. It is never recorded, never saved, and never sent anywhere. If you decline the microphone permission, everything else in the app still works.

On your device

Your lesson progress, quiz streak, points and settings are stored on your device, in the app’s own storage. Without an account, guitarRot does not upload them and never receives them. Uninstalling the app (or clearing the website’s data in your browser) deletes them.

Your phone may include the app’s data in its own backup: Google backup on Android, iCloud backup on iPhone. That is how progress can come back on a new phone even without an account. The backup is made by your phone, not by guitarRot: it follows your phone’s backup settings (you can switch it off there), it is stored by Google or Apple, and it is never sent to us.

If you create an account (optional)

An account lets your progress follow you to a new phone. You can sign up with a username, an email address and a password, or with Google or Apple. If you do, we store:

  • your username. It is unique, so anyone can check whether a name is taken, and it is the name other players see if you join the leaderboard;
  • your email address;
  • with Google sign-in, the name and profile picture your Google account shares with the app; with Apple, the name you choose to share, and either your email or, if you hide it, a private relay address Apple forwards to you (we never receive your Google or Apple password);
  • a sign-in password, kept only as a hash by Firebase Authentication, never readable by us;
  • your progress (lessons done, points, streaks, quiz bests, settings), in our cloud database;
  • if you join the leaderboard, which is its own choice: the name you pick for it and your points, which other players can see. Nothing else about you is shown to anyone.

To protect sign-up and sign-in from abuse, Firebase Authentication also records the IP address and device details of each sign-in; Google keeps those logs for a few weeks.

We use this only to run your account: to sign you in, to keep your progress, to show the leaderboard if you joined it, and to keep accounts secure. The legal basis is the contract between us for the account you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), and, for the security logs, our legitimate interest in keeping the service safe (Art. 6(1)(f)). You can sign out at any time and keep playing without an account. When you create an account you agree to our terms of use, which set the rules for usernames and leaderboard names.

Usage statistics (optional)

The first time you use the app it asks whether you would like to share usage statistics, and you can change your answer at any time in Settings, under Share usage statistics. Until you say yes, nothing is collected and the analytics code is not even loaded. If you say yes, the app sends Google Analytics:

  • which screens you open (a tab, a song, a lesson, the tuner, Settings) and how long you stay;
  • what you do there, as simple events: a lesson started or finished, a song played, a quiz run finished with its score, a pick unlocked, a level reached, the instrument you switch to;
  • your instrument and a rough band of your level (like “3 to 5”);
  • what Google adds on its own: the app or browser version, device type, language, and an approximate place (country or city) worked out from your connection.

It is tied to a random identifier on your device, not to your account: your name, email, account id, progress, anything you type, and anything from the microphone are never part of it. We use it only to see which parts of guitarRot are used and where to improve it. There is no advertising: advertising features, ad personalisation and advertising identifiers are off, Google signals are off, and the data is never sold or shared with advertisers. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time with the same switch; withdrawing does not affect what was collected before.

Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically. Switching the setting off stops collection straight away and removes the random identifier from your device (events already sent can take a few seconds to arrive, and are then kept for that long). Because the identifier is random and not linked to your account, we cannot pick one person’s analytics out of the rest afterwards; it simply expires.

Cookies and storage on the website

The website keeps your progress and settings in your browser’s local storage, and, when you are signed in, your sign-in session in its IndexedDB storage. Both are needed for the features you use and are never read by anyone else. There are no advertising or tracking cookies. Only if you say yes to usage statistics does Google Analytics set its cookies (_ga and _ga_<id>), which hold the random identifier and last up to 13 months. Saying no, or switching it off later, deletes them.

If you report a name

Every other player’s row on the leaderboard has Report and Hide. Report opens an email to us with the reported name and that player’s id; it comes from your own email address, so we see that too. We use it only to review the name and to reply to you, on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the leaderboard safe (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), and we delete the email once the report is handled, at the latest after 6 months. Hide only stores the player’s id on your device: we never receive that list, and uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it. We never tell the reported player who reported them.

Who handles your data

  • Google (Firebase). Accounts, the cloud database and analytics run on Google’s Firebase (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Google Analytics). Google is our processor under its data processing terms and only acts on our instructions.
  • Vercel hosts the website and keeps ordinary server logs (address, time, page), as any web host does.
  • Proton (Switzerland) hosts our email inbox, so emails you send us are stored there.
  • Google and Apple sign-in. If you sign in with Google or Apple, that company checks who you are under its own privacy policy and tells us only what is listed above.
  • App stores. Google Play and the App Store handle downloads, updates and crash reports from your phone under their own policies.

We do not use any other service, and we do not sell or share your data with anyone else.

Where your data is stored

The cloud database (your progress, username and leaderboard entry) is in the European Union, in Google’s Europe multi-region (Belgium and the Netherlands). Firebase Authentication runs only from Google’s data centres in the United States, so your email, sign-in details and sign-in logs are processed there, and Google may process analytics data in the United States too. Vercel’s servers are worldwide. These transfers are covered by the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, under which Google and Vercel are certified, and by the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses in their data processing terms. Switzerland, where Proton is, has an EU adequacy decision.

How long we keep it

  • Account, username, synced progress, leaderboard entry: for as long as the account exists. Deleting the account removes them at once.
  • Sign-in security logs: a few weeks (Google’s schedule).
  • Usage statistics: up to 14 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Report emails: until the report is handled, at the latest 6 months.
  • Other emails you send us: as long as we need to answer them, at the latest 12 months.

Deleting your data

  • Account and cloud data: in the app, open Settings, then Account, then Delete account. That deletes your sign-in, your username and email, your synced progress and your leaderboard entry for good. If you signed up with Apple, Apple asks you to confirm first and guitarRot’s access to your Apple ID is revoked too. Progress on your phone stays, and you can keep playing without an account.
  • Without the app: email guitar.rot@proton.me from the address on your account (or tell us your username) and ask us to delete it. We delete the same data as above within 30 days and reply when it is done.
  • Usage data: switch Share usage statistics off in Settings; what was already collected expires on its own, after at most 14 months.
  • On your device: uninstall the app, or clear its data in your phone’s or browser’s settings.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us, at any time and free of charge:

  • for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, including in a machine-readable form you can take elsewhere;
  • to correct it (you can also change your username in the app);
  • to delete it, or to restrict how we use it;
  • to stop using it where we rely on our legitimate interest (the security logs and reports);
  • to withdraw a consent you gave (switching usage statistics off does that).

Email guitar.rot@proton.me, ideally from the address on your account so we know the request is yours. We answer within one month. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority; in Romania that is ANSPDCP (dataprotection.ro), and you can also go to the authority where you live.

Giving us any personal data is optional: without an account and without usage statistics, the whole app still works. An account needs an email address or a Google or Apple sign-in, and a username. We make no automated decisions about you; the leaderboard only sorts points.

Network

The Android and iPhone apps work offline: all lessons, sounds and songs are inside the app. They only connect to Google when you have an account (to sign in and keep your progress) or have said yes to usage statistics. When you tap Share on a riff or song, the app opens your phone’s own share sheet with a link that contains only the name of that riff or song. You choose which app, if any, receives it; guitarRot does not see where it goes. Links in Settings, such as this policy, open in your phone’s browser.

Children

guitarRot is for anyone who wants to learn, and the app has no ads. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. Anyone can use the whole app with no account and no sharing. If you are under 16, please ask a parent or guardian before you create an account or say yes to usage statistics. If you think a child has given us information they should not have, tell us and we will delete it.
