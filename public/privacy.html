The microphone

The tuner and the “play this note” checks in lessons listen through your microphone so the app can tell which note your instrument is playing. The microphone only turns on after you tap Start tuner or Listen for me, and it turns off when you leave that screen.

The sound is analysed on your device, a few times a second, to estimate its pitch. It is never recorded, never saved, and never sent anywhere. If you decline the microphone permission, everything else in the app still works.

On your device

Your lesson progress, quiz streak, points and settings are stored on your device, in the app’s own storage. Without an account, guitarRot does not upload them and never receives them. Uninstalling the app deletes the copy on your phone.

Android may also include the app’s data in your phone’s own Google backup, which is how your progress can come back when you move to a new phone. That backup is made by Android, not by guitarRot: it is encrypted, it is stored by Google, it follows your phone’s backup settings (you can switch it off there), and it is never sent to us.

If you create an account (optional)

An account lets your progress follow you to a new phone. You can sign up with a username, an email address and a password, or with Google or Apple. If you do, we store:

  • your username. It is unique, so anyone can check whether a name is taken, and it is the name other players see if you join the leaderboard;
  • your email address;
  • with Google sign-in, the name and profile picture your Google account shares with the app; with Apple, the name you choose to share, and either your email or, if you hide it, a private relay address Apple forwards to you (we never receive your Google or Apple password);
  • a sign-in password, kept only as a hash by Firebase Authentication, never readable by us;
  • your progress (lessons done, points, streaks, quiz bests, settings), in our cloud database;
  • if you join the leaderboard, which is its own choice: the name you pick for it and your points, which other players can see. Nothing else about you is shown to anyone.

We use this only to run your account: to sign you in, to keep your progress, and to show the leaderboard if you joined it. You can sign out at any time and keep playing without an account.

Anonymous usage (optional)

The first time you use the app it asks whether you would like to share anonymous usage, and you can change your answer at any time in Settings, under Share anonymous usage. Until you say yes, nothing is collected and the analytics code is not even loaded. If you say yes, the app sends Google Analytics:

  • which screens you open (a tab, a song, a lesson, the tuner, Settings) and how long you stay;
  • what you do there, as simple events: a lesson started or finished, a song played, a quiz run finished with its score, a pick unlocked, a level reached, the instrument you switch to;
  • your instrument and a rough band of your level (like “3 to 5”);
  • what Google adds on its own: the app or browser version, device type, language, and an approximate place (country or city) worked out from your connection.

It is tied to a random identifier on your device, not to your account: your name, email, account id, progress, anything you type, and anything from the microphone are never part of it. We use it only to see which parts of guitarRot are used and where to improve it. There is no advertising: advertising features, ad personalisation and advertising identifiers are off, and the data is never sold or shared with advertisers.

Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically. Switching the setting off stops collection straight away and removes the random identifier from your device (events already sent can take a few seconds to arrive, and are then kept for that long). Because the identifier is random and not linked to your account, we cannot pick one person’s analytics out of the rest afterwards; it simply expires.

Who handles your data

Accounts, the cloud database and analytics run on Google’s Firebase, so Google acts as our processor for them (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Google Analytics). If you sign in with Google or Apple, that company checks who you are under its own privacy policy and tells us only what is listed above. The cloud database is hosted in Europe. Google may process analytics data in other countries, under its standard data protection terms. The website you may be reading this on is hosted by Vercel, which keeps ordinary server logs (address, time, page) as any web host does. We do not use any other service, and we do not sell or share your data with anyone else.

Deleting your data

  • Account and cloud data: in the app, open Settings, then Account, then Delete account. That deletes your sign-in, your username and email, your synced progress and your leaderboard entry for good. Progress on your phone stays, and you can keep playing without an account.
  • Without the app: email guitar.rot@proton.me from the address on your account (or tell us your username) and ask us to delete it. We delete the same data as above within 30 days and reply when it is done.
  • Usage data: switch Share anonymous usage off in Settings; what was already collected expires on its own, after at most 14 months.
  • On your device: uninstall the app, or clear its data in your phone’s settings.

If you live in the EU or the UK you also have the right to see, correct and delete your data, to withdraw a consent you gave (switching analytics off does that), and to complain to your data protection authority. Using the steps above is the quickest way to exercise most of these; for anything else, get in touch as described at the end of this page.

Network

The Android app works offline: all lessons, sounds and songs are inside the app. It only connects to Google when you have an account (to sign in and keep your progress) or have said yes to usage sharing. When you tap Share on a riff or song, the app opens your phone’s own share sheet with a link that contains only the name of that riff or song. You choose which app, if any, receives it; guitarRot does not see where it goes. Links in Settings, such as this policy, open in your phone’s browser.

Children

guitarRot is for anyone who wants to learn, and the app has no ads. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. Anyone can use the whole app with no account and no sharing. If you are under 16, please ask a parent or guardian before you create an account or say yes to usage sharing. If you think a child has given us information they should not have, tell us and we will delete it.
