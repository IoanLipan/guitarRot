guitarRot

Privacy policy

Effective 18 October 2026

guitarRot teaches guitar. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not collect, store on any server, sell or share personal data about you.

The microphone

The tuner and the “play this note” checks in lessons listen through your microphone so the app can tell which note your guitar is playing. The microphone only turns on after you tap Start tuner or Listen for me, and it turns off when you leave that screen.

The sound is analysed on your device, a few times a second, to estimate its pitch. It is never recorded, never saved, and never sent anywhere. If you decline the microphone permission, everything else in the app still works.

Your progress and settings

Your lesson progress, quiz streak and settings are stored on your device, in the app’s own storage. guitarRot does not upload them and never receives them. Uninstalling the app deletes the copy on your phone.

Android may also include the app’s data in your phone’s own Google backup, which is how your progress can come back when you move to a new phone. That backup is made by Android, not by guitarRot: it is encrypted, it is stored by Google, it follows your phone’s backup settings (you can switch it off there), and it is never sent to us.

Network

The Android app works offline: all lessons, sounds and songs are inside the app. When you tap Share on a riff or song, the app opens your phone’s own share sheet with a link that contains only the name of that riff or song. You choose which app, if any, receives it; guitarRot does not see where it goes. Links in Settings, such as this policy, open in your phone’s browser.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/IoanLipan/guitarRot/issues.
