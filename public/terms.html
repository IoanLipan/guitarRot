What guitarRot is

guitarRot is an app for learning guitar and ukulele: lessons, songs, quizzes, a tuner and a practice streak. It is free, it has no ads, and the whole app works with no account. The lessons and songs are written for guitarRot. They are there to help you learn, and they do not replace a teacher.

Reporting and hiding players

On the leaderboard, the dots at the end of another player’s row open Report this name and Hide this player.

  • Report: opens an email to guitar.rot@proton.me with the name and the player’s id, and hides that player for you too. You can also write to us yourself, at any time, about any name or any behaviour.
  • Hide: takes a player off your board, on your phone. It is kept on your phone only, and you can bring everyone back from the foot of the board.

We aim to look at every report and answer within 24 to 48 hours, and to act on a name that breaks these rules as soon as we have looked at it.
